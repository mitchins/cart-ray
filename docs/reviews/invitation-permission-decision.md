# Review invitation permission

Squirrel's automatic-invitation option is checked by default and can be cleared before checkout. A merchant can choose the initial state. The disclosure permits one non-promotional review invitation about the purchase; it does not cover marketing, newsletters, incentives, or other communications. The server records the final choice, configured default, disclosure wording and version, and time against the order. A preselected choice is recorded as such and is not described as express opt-in. Confirmed payment, the trusted purchase contact, current merchant settings, and suppression checks remain necessary before sending.

The invitation identifies the merchant, gives contact details, and offers a functional one-click unsubscribe. Receipt-only links are a separate, later option; Stripe's standard receipt is not assumed to support them.

## Australian compliance caveat

This product choice does not determine whether a particular invitation is a commercial electronic message or whether permission is sufficient in its circumstances. If the Spam Act applies, the sender must establish an appropriate basis for sending and meet identification and unsubscribe requirements. ACMA describes express and inferred consent separately and notes that inferred consent depends on the relationship and reasonable expectations; a prechecked box is not evidence that the customer actively selected it. The merchant must assess the actual message, relationship and applicable jurisdiction before live sending. See [ACMA: Avoid sending spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam) and [ACMA: Email and SMS unsubscribe rules](https://www.acma.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-05/Fact%20sheet%20-%20email%20and%20SMS%20unsubscribe%20rules.pdf).
