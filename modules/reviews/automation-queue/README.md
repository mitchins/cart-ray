# Synthetic due-event consumer

This optional consumer handles **test-only** messages `{acquisition_id, claim_token}` from a private queue. Its service binding points to the existing test Reviews Worker. The Worker independently checks its store/environment and persisted due time, and claims a held intent with the durable token. A held receipt acknowledges evaluation only: it is never permission to create or send an invitation.

Enable both `REVIEWS_ENVIRONMENT=test` and `REVIEWS_AUTOMATION_QUEUE=test`, configure a distinct synthetic `REVIEWS_AUTOMATION_TOKEN` matching the Reviews automation ingress token, and bind `REVIEWS_API` to that Worker. Configure a finite maximum delivery count and dead-letter queue. Invalid message shapes are discarded; unavailable, premature or failed evaluations retry after 60 seconds and ultimately require investigation. Messages contain no recipient or review data. The trusted test producer must persist one unpredictable token per intent and reuse it on every retry or redelivery. Do not expose queue production to untrusted callers.

The synthetic producer must enqueue at the authoritative persisted `due_at` using bounded queue delivery delay, including an explicit strategy for delays exceeding the provider limit; early arrivals retry and must not be mistaken for authorization. This increment has no production producer, timer, scheduler, outbound email, reminder, or claim that normal purchase automation is production-ready. Recipient/consent evidence remains held, not delivery authority. Provider scheduling limits, queue deployment, event authentication, clock, activation, backfill and eligibility policies remain qualification gates.
